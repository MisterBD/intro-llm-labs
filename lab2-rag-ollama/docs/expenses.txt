Acme Expense Rules (fictional sample document)

Meal allowance: the per-day meal allowance is 60 EUR in Europe, 75 USD in North America, and 8000 JPY in Japan. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Hotels: the nightly hotel cap is 180 EUR in most European cities and 250 EUR in Paris, London, Geneva and Zurich. Breakfast should be included in the room rate when possible.

Receipts: any expense above 25 EUR requires an itemised receipt. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the trip will not be reimbursed.

Ground transport: taxis are allowed for early-morning or late-night transfers. For everything else, public transport or a shared ride is expected. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at 0.35 EUR per kilometre.
